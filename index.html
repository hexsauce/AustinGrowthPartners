The leak is almost never the marketing.

Six of the eight answered, most of them quickly. They asked sensible questions. Then they let me hang up without ever asking who I was.

That is the part an owner never sees. A booked job leaves a record. A call that went nowhere leaves nothing, so it never comes up, and every month it happens again.

Not one of the eight had anything installed that could connect a booked job back to whatever produced it. They are all paying for something — Yelp, HomeAdvisor, Angi, Google — and not one of them could tell you which of it works. Neither could you, and that is the first thing I would fix.
